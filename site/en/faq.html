Frequently asked questions

Everything to know before buying a painting, a print or a goodie.

How do I buy an original painting?

In the gallery, click “Buy the original” under the work you like. A pre-filled email opens: send it and I will reply within 48 hours with payment and delivery details.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer and card payment through a secure link. Originals from €1,000 can be paid in three interest-free instalments.

Do you ship outside Belgium?

Yes, across Europe and worldwide. Every work is packed to measure and insured in transit. Allow 3 to 5 working days in Belgium, 5 to 10 in Europe.

Do originals come with a certificate?

Yes. Every original is signed and comes with a dated certificate of authenticity signed by hand.

What is the difference between an original and a print?

The original is the hand-painted canvas, a one-off piece. The print is a quality art print on 230 g matte paper, in A4, A3 or A2, sold in my Etsy shop.

Do you take commissions?

Yes, gladly: a portrait of your pet, a size made for your home… Write to me with your idea. A commission usually takes 6 to 10 weeks.

Can I see the works in person before buying?

Yes, by appointment at the studio, or at the exhibitions announced in the newsletter.

Can I return a painting?

You have 14 days after delivery to change your mind. The work must come back in its original packaging; I refund you as soon as it arrives.

Who handles the goodies?

T-shirts, hoodies and frames are printed and shipped by Redbubble. For delivery or sizing questions, Redbubble’s customer service will help you directly.
