The Art of Guido De Oliveira.

Through our history as a civilisation and the uncertainty of the future, through wildlife, objects charged with memory and cosmic visions, Guido de Oliveira explores the relationship between humankind, nature and a world in constant transformation. His works invite you to witness his vision of the world while prompting reflection on the climate, the effects of human activity and the fragility of life.
