About

Born in 1976, Guido de Oliveira is a painter of Portuguese origin with German roots, now based in Brussels. His practice has been shaped since childhood by watercolour, acrylic and constant learning through experimentation. His travels and influences, from surrealism to hyperrealism, feed a personal world where precision, imagination and sensitivity meet. He has worked as a professional artist since 2015.
