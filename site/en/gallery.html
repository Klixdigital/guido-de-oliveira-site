The Cosmic Cat

Technique
Acrylic on canvas
Dimensions
50 × 70 cm
Year
2024

A feline figure at the heart of a flowering night landscape. The animal, a recurring motif for the artist, becomes an almost cosmic presence, at once wild, contemplative and mysterious.

The story behind the work

This canvas is an ode to my love of felines. At first, a friend told me he would love to see a painting of a wild cat he had seen in a photo. But his wish soon faded away, turning into a personal artistic adventure for me.

Wave at Sunset

Technique
Acrylic on canvas
Dimensions
40 × 40 cm
Year
2024

A vision of the sea where sunset, wave and sky merge in a movement of transformation. Memories of Portuguese beaches come through in an intense palette and an almost planetary energy.

The story behind the work

After spending an eternity of summers wandering along Portuguese beaches, it is hardly surprising that the sea and its waves ended up leaving a deep mark on me.
